PawPaw Power

PawPaw Power is an original retro side-scrolling platformer where the player controls a cute calico cat hero through colorful worlds, collects cat food, uses power-ups, throws fishbones with the Space key, avoids hazards, discovers secret routes, activates checkpoints, defeats enemies, and beats a boss at the end of every level to continue the adventure.

Game Objects and What They Mean

Calico Cat Hero

The player controls PawPaw to run, jump, dodge, collect, and fight.

Cat Food

Collect it for score, rewards, routes, and extra progress.

Fishbone

Throw it to hit enemies, open boss weak spots, and survive danger.

Blocks

Hit mystery, breakable, or hidden blocks to find rewards and secrets.

Hazards

Avoid spikes, cars, rivers, lava, falling objects, and boss attacks.

Bosses

Defeat the boss at the end of each level to unlock the next stage.

All Cat Food and Power-Ups

Normal Cat Food

Collect for score. Trails also guide jumps, routes, and safe paths.

Red Cat Food

Gives a temporary speed boost. Use it to run faster and clear longer sections.

Blue Cat Food

Gives a shield. It protects PawPaw from one hit.

Purple Cat Food

Gives temporary double jump. Use it for high platforms and recovery.

Gold Cat Food

Gives a large score bonus. Often found in risky or secret areas.

Green Cat Food

Gives an extra life. It is rare and valuable.

Silver Cat Food

Upgrades fishbone throws for a short time. Very useful against bosses.

All Blocks and Interactions

Solid Blocks

Stand on them, jump from them, and use them as normal platforms.

Mystery Blocks

Hit from below to release cat food, power-ups, or hidden rewards.

Breakable Blocks

Break them from below or with attacks to open paths and reveal items.

Hidden Blocks

Invisible until PawPaw touches them from below. Search for secrets.

Used Blocks

Show that a mystery block reward has already been collected.

Secret Path Blocks

Reveal shortcuts, bonus rooms, or hidden routes when discovered.

All Enemies

Walking Enemy

Patrols platforms. Stomp it from above or hit it with a fishbone.

Flying Enemy

Moves through the air. Dodge its path or throw fishbones to clear it.

Jumping Enemy

Hops in rhythm. Time a stomp or attack from a safe distance.

Spiky Enemy

Do not stomp it. Avoid it or attack with fishbones when possible.

Traffic Enemy

Moves fast in city areas. Watch its timing before crossing.

River Enemy

Appears near water. Keep balance on platforms while avoiding it.

Rooftop Enemy

Blocks rooftop paths. Stomp carefully or throw a fishbone.

All Platforms and Routes

Ground Platform

Normal safe ground for running, landing, and fighting enemies.

Floating Platform

Jump onto it to cross gaps or reach higher food trails.

Moving Platform

Ride it and jump at the right time to continue safely.

Rooftop Platform

Use it for building jumps, ledges, balconies, and vertical routes.

Log Platform

Ride it over river water without falling in.

Boat Platform

Use it as a moving river crossing platform.

Traffic Safe Island

Stand here between road lanes while waiting for traffic gaps.

Tower Platform

Used in the final level for harder jumps and collapsing routes.

Secrets, Checkpoints, and Goals

Checkpoint

Touch it to respawn there after losing a life.

Goal Object

Marks level progress, but bosses must be defeated to finish levels.

Cardboard Box Tunnel

Enter it to reach a bonus route or shortcut.

Cat Door

Use it to enter secret spaces or hidden paths.

Tree Hole

Find it in grassland areas to reach secret rewards.

Drain

City shortcut entrance for hidden routes.

Rooftop Vent

Rooftop portal or shortcut into secret areas.

All Hazards

Pits

Falling into a pit costs a life. Jump carefully across gaps.

Spikes

Touching spikes hurts PawPaw unless shield is active.

Cars

Move across road lanes. Touching them causes life loss.

Buses

Larger road hazards. Wait for timing gaps before crossing.

Bikes

Fast moving traffic hazards. Watch their speed before jumping.

River Water

Falling into water costs a life. Use logs, boats, and platforms.

Fire

Damages PawPaw. Jump over it or use shield protection.

Lava

Instant life-loss hazard in the final tower. Avoid completely.

Falling Rocks

Drop from above. Move quickly or wait for a safe opening.

Falling Signs

Rooftop hazards that fall vertically. Time your run underneath.

Moving Obstacles

Move in patterns. Observe the motion and pass at the right time.

Collapsing Platforms

Fall or disappear after contact. Keep moving after landing.

Boss Projectiles

Boss attacks that must be dodged before counterattacking.

All Bosses

Grumpy Garden Mole

Level 1 boss. Dodge dirt attacks, throw fishbones, and jump on weak openings.

Traffic Rat Rider

Level 2 boss. Avoid scooter passes and traffic patterns, then throw fishbones when it slows.

Rooftop Crow

Level 3 boss. Dodge falling objects and hit it with fishbones during flight windows.

River Croc

Level 4 boss. Stay on floating platforms and attack when its weak point opens.

Shadow Cat King

Final boss. Dodge dark projectiles, survive phases, use power-ups, and keep throwing fishbones.

Training Dummy Boss

Tutorial boss. Practice dodging, throwing fishbones, reading the health bar, and finishing the fight.
